About Resume Screener

Resume Screener was invented by Ajay, who built it after watching hiring teams lose hours manually comparing resumes against job descriptions. The idea was simple: give recruiters and hopeful candidates alike a fast, honest read on how well a resume lines up with a role, without needing a spreadsheet or a third-party recruiter tool.

The website works by pulling the meaningful keywords out of a job description and checking how many of them show up in the uploaded resume. It then turns that overlap into a clear match score, a ring you can read at a glance, and two simple lists — what matched, and what's missing — so you know exactly where a resume stands and what to improve.

It's built to be lightweight and private: everything happens right in the browser, there's no account to create, and no resume data is stored anywhere. Whether you're a hiring manager screening a stack of applicants or a job seeker checking your own resume before you apply, Resume Screener is meant to give you that answer in seconds, not minutes.
