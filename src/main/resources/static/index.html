How it works

No account, no onboarding call, no CSV templates to fill out. Resume Screener reads the job description the way a hiring manager would — pulling out the must-have skills, years of experience, and role-specific keywords — then checks the resume against that list line by line.

1

Upload the resume

2

In seconds you'll see a match score, the skills that lined up, and the gaps worth asking about in a screening call.

Built for the first pass, not the final call

Resume Screener isn't trying to replace a recruiter's judgment — it's built to handle the repetitive part of the job: skimming a hundred resumes to find the twelve worth a closer look. Teams use it to triage inbound applications, sanity-check a shortlist before a hiring huddle, or get a second read on a resume that's hard to evaluate at a glance. The output is a starting point for a conversation, not a verdict — every score comes with the matched and missing skills laid out so you can see exactly why it landed where it did.
