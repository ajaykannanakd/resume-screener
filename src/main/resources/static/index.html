    About Resume Screener

    Resume Screener was invented by Ajay, who built it after watching hiring teams lose hours manually comparing resumes against job requirements. The idea was simple: give recruiters and hopeful candidates alike a fast, honest read on how well a resume lines up with a role, without needing a spreadsheet or a third-party recruiter tool.

    The website works by matching the target role you enter against a set of typical requirements for that kind of position — core skills, tools, years of experience, and education level — and checking how many of them show up in the uploaded resume. It then turns that overlap into a clear match score and a plain-English list of what qualifies the candidate and what's missing.

    It's built to be lightweight and private: everything happens right in the browser, there's no account to create, and no resume data is stored anywhere. Whether you're a hiring manager screening a stack of applicants or a job seeker checking your own resume before you apply, Resume Screener is meant to give you that answer in seconds, not minutes.
